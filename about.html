Manitham Hospital | About Us

Where Compassion Became a Movement.

Any big achievement starts with the small step and it will be eventually joined and followed by a community, Here, Dr. A Selvakumar, who has hailed from a deep rural community from Salem district, has set his goal to become a doctor, after seeing his father’s demise without proper medical care in his surroundings, and extended his dream to serve the poor and needy circle, wherein modern, life style diseases are costly dream. He opened a small clinic in the year 2016 and his hard work, determination, consistency, honesty made him to be successful with 16 clinics, in and around Salem district and a hospital in Perumbur, Chennai under the banner of Yazhini group of Hospitals and Manitham Hospital in the span of 10 years. The name of the hospital proves the ideology of the CEO and his service to the human kind. To serve a human is a greater than a service to a God, which is a basic philosophy of a CEO and his team, and they prove the same in everyday life with humility and humanity.

Our Founder’s Promise to You

Dear Patient / Family Member,
This Yazhini Group of Hospital and Manitham Hospital is a great means for me to serve this society. Being a doctor from the rural background and graduated from Kilpauk Medical College and Hospital, Chennai, have always felt the pain of a patient from economically weaker group and innocence and unawareness of the severity of a disease and its approach. It’s my promise and moral responsibility for this profession, assure you that whoever come for the treatment for whichever disease, would be given utmost care comfort and compassion by our team, irrespective of their economic background, social status etc. As a Founder and Chairman in this ecosystem, I guarantee and value your precious time, money and emotions. As a part of this group, you will experience stress-free, personalized care with compassion, dignity and respect. I am committed to offer the best, quality treatment with empathy which make you to feel a long-time relationship with this group for any of your needs.
Thank you for your time
Sincerely,
Dr A Selvakumar
Founder & Chairman,
